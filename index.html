Step 6 • Multi-channel A/B Strategy

Start With 1, Scale Later — Testing Plan

If you launch 5 channels at once, your time gets split 5 ways and none of them will take off. The right way: Prove the format on Channel A over 90 days / 20–25 videos, then launch B and C with the winning format.

How does the money reach Bangladesh?

AdSense → Directly to your bank account (wire transfer, paid once you reach $100). Payoneer is needed for affiliate income (Amazon, etc.), not for AdSense.

Why do cartoons earn less despite more views?

Personalized ads are disabled on kids' content, and advertisers pay less. So even 1 million views can earn less than 100K views on an SEO video.
